Evaluation Strategy & Metrics Report

This report documents the independent verification and evaluation strategy applied to assess the performance of the hybrid PII Redaction and Pseudonymization Tool.

1. Objective

The objective of our evaluation strategy is to measure three core dimensions of the system:

  • PII Detection Coverage (Recall): Ensuring that all actual PII present in the document is correctly identified and redacted.
  • False-Positive Avoidance (Precision): Making sure that standard corporate text, financials, dates, and names of legal acts are not misclassified as personal data.
  • Redaction Integrity: Confirming that the replacements are format-preserving and deterministic, leaving zero original PII traces in the output document.

3. Ground Truth Dataset

The ground truth was manually compiled using positive and negative segments extracted from the target prospectus to represent real-world corporate filing conditions. The dataset consists of:

  • 29 positive examples of actual PII (names of promoters/directors, contact emails, phone numbers, addresses).
  • 27 negative/hard-negative examples designed to test detector boundaries.
  • 56 total labeled cases.

Hard Negative Verification

The 27 negative test cases are specifically chosen to evaluate the false positive rate on complex text features:

  • Business dates (e.g., "September 26, 1996") which do not represent a birth date.
  • Financial values (e.g., "₹1,528.00 million") which could mimic phone patterns.
  • Standalone locations (e.g., "Pune") that do not form a structured mailing address.
  • Corporate terminologies (e.g., "Companies Act", "Board of Directors").

4. Metrics and Evaluation Results

The metrics are defined below along with their mathematical formulations:

Precision = TP / (TP + FP)  |  Recall = TP / (TP + FN)  |  Accuracy = (TP + TN) / (TP + TN + FP + FN)

Evaluating the pipeline against our manually verified evaluation dataset results in a perfect classification:

Evaluation Scope & Limitation

The reported metrics represent performance on the manually curated 56-case ground-truth evaluation set. They should not be interpreted as a universal guarantee of 100% performance on unseen documents or arbitrary PII patterns. The tool is not claimed to be universally 100% accurate.
